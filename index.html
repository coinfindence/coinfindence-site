Four key engines.
One unified system.

Each engine is designed for a specific market condition. That means fewer forced trades, cleaner execution logic, and a system that adapts instead of pretending every chart behaves the same way.

Engine A

Range / Retest

A

Built for range conditions and retest structures. Designed to create lower-risk entries while reducing the chance of being repeatedly chopped inside sideways markets.

  • Focuses on controlled reactions from key zones
  • Suited for range-bound and retest-heavy environments
  • Aims to reduce unnecessary exposure during choppy price action
Use when market structure is present, but expansion has not fully emerged.
Engine B1

Trend Continuation

B1

Designed to capture the continuation leg of an already established trend. Combines structure and momentum assessment to target the most stable directional trades.

  • Built for structured pullback and continuation conditions
  • Filters for cleaner directional flow
  • Prioritizes stable trend-following execution over random chasing
Use when the trend is already formed and the market offers a qualified continuation setup.
Engine B2

Momentum Expansion

B2

Built for acceleration phases. This engine focuses on fast expansion after valid breaks, aiming to capture stronger directional pushes when the market shifts into momentum mode.

  • Designed for breakout-to-expansion sequences
  • Targets stronger velocity after structure release
  • Fits more aggressive conditions while preserving execution rules
Use when structure breaks cleanly and momentum begins to accelerate instead of fade.
